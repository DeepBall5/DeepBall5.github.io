# Hook & Ladder

The daily NFL trivia game where the obvious answer is a kneel-down.

Seven plays, a 25-second play clock, and rare answers gain big yards. Reach field goal range or punch it in for a touchdown. New drive every day, plus an archive of past drives and unlimited practice.

Each drive draws seven questions from a bank of about 180 (players, teams, season and career milestones, draft-day oddities, Super Bowl history, X's and O's), with no formula more than twice per drive. After the final whistle you see how you stack up against thousands of simulated casual fans who played the same drive.

Play at **https://deepball5.github.io**

Everything runs in one file (`index.html`); progress and stats are saved in each player's browser.

Every correct answer counts. Player, team and coach questions are checked against every player in NFL, AFL and AAFC history (1920 to today) and every head coach since 2000, from the public [nflverse](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data) roster, schedule and draft data. Closed lists (Super Bowl results, awards, the Hall of Fame, penalties, nicknamed plays) are checked against their official or published lists.
